# NEXUS paper title options

These three paper titles have been considered. The first is the current recommendation
and is shown on the public project page.

## Recommended and current public title

**NEXUS: A Perceptive Foundation Policy for Cross-Domain Whole-Body Teleoperation**

This phrasing makes the technical object explicit: NEXUS is an action-producing policy,
not only a general representation model. **Perceptive** directly modifies **Foundation
Policy**, while **Cross-Domain Whole-Body Teleoperation** states the application and
transfer setting.

Use **Foundation Policy** rather than **Foundation Model** because the contribution is a
generalizable control policy that maps observations and human motion to whole-body robot
actions. The broader term **Foundation Model** could imply a reusable representation or
backbone intended for many unrelated downstream systems.

The foundation-policy claim should be supported by one policy covering diverse motions,
terrains, and domains, with evidence of generalization or adaptation beyond individual
training behaviors.

## Previous descriptive title

**NEXUS: Cross-Domain Perceptive Whole-Body Teleoperation for Rich Terrain-Adaptive
Behaviors**

This version foregrounds the concrete capabilities: cross-domain teleoperation,
perception, whole-body control, and rich terrain-adaptive behaviors. It is more
conservative but gives less emphasis to the unified reusable policy.

## Earlier foundation-policy alternative

**NEXUS: A Foundation Policy for Cross-Domain Perceptive Whole-Body Teleoperation**

This version introduced the foundation-policy framing, but **perceptive** can be read as
modifying teleoperation rather than the policy. The recommended title above resolves that
ambiguity.

## Shared naming rationale

The name **NEXUS** represents the connections established by the work:

- between humans and humanoid robots;
- between flat-ground and terrain-aware motion;
- between perception and decision-making; and
- between perceptive locomotion and whole-body motion tracking.

The project name and URL remain **NEXUS** and `https://nexus-humanoid.github.io/`
regardless of which paper title is selected.
